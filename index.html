Typo Check for Word

A Microsoft Word add-in that flags typos, grammar slips, banned words and style patterns in the open document. All checking runs inside the browser.

For IT review

  • Source files: github.com/esthergonswork/TypoCheck
  • Manifest: manifest.xml
  • Permission requested: ReadWriteDocument, so fixes can be applied to the open document.
  • External scripts: Microsoft's own office.js from appsforoffice.microsoft.com. Nothing else.
  • Network calls: none. The dictionary is built into taskpane.html and no document text leaves the browser.
  • Storage: the user's word lists and settings in the browser's local storage.

Deployment

Microsoft 365 admin center › Settings › Integrated apps › Upload custom apps › Office Add-in. Upload manifest.xml and assign it to the requesting users.
